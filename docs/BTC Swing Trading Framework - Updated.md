# BTC Swing Trading Framework

## Overview

This framework outlines a structured Bitcoin swing trading strategy focused on:

- Support and resistance
- Volume profile analysis
- Range trading
- Multi-timeframe analysis
- Trendline confluence
- Light fundamental/on-chain/derivatives bias overlay
- High-probability leveraged trades
- Patience and selective execution

The objective is not constant trading.

The objective is:
- To wait patiently for high-probability setups
- To trade only when multiple factors align
- To capture medium-sized directional moves during the trading week
- To preserve capital and avoid unnecessary exposure

---

# Core Philosophy

The strategy is based around:

1. Patience
2. Market structure
3. Support and resistance
4. Volume profile confluence
5. Trend continuation and rejection
6. Risk management
7. Low trade frequency

9. Trading reactions, not predictions

# Weekly Trading Cycle

## Friday / Weekend Strategy

The market often becomes:
- Lower liquidity
- More erratic
- Less reliable
- More prone to fake moves

The framework avoids unnecessary weekend exposure.

### Thursday / Friday
- Reduce exposure where appropriate
- Close trades if market conditions weaken
- Avoid forcing new setups
- Prepare levels for the next week

### Saturday
- Observation only
- No emotional trades
- Build possible scenarios
- Mark key levels

### Sunday (10 PM GMT)
- Futures/opening liquidity returns
- Begin analysing market structure
- Look for potential setups
- Decide whether conditions justify a trade

# Fundamental, On-Chain & Derivatives Bias Overlay

The framework is primarily a technical-analysis framework.

The core trade decision still comes from:
- Support and resistance
- Volume profile
- Market structure
- Range analysis
- Acceptance and rejection
- Multi-timeframe confirmation
- Defined invalidation
- Controlled risk

However, Bitcoin is strongly affected by:
- Institutional flows
- Miner economics
- Long-term holder behaviour
- Perpetual futures positioning
- Leverage build-up and flushes

Therefore, a light fundamental/on-chain/derivatives overlay should be used to improve weekly bias and trade filtering.

This overlay is not an entry system.

It should never replace technical confirmation.

## Core Rule

Technical analysis decides the trade.

Fundamental, on-chain, and derivatives data adjusts:
- Bias
- Confidence
- Caution
- Position quality
- Position size
- Willingness to wait

If the technical setup is strong and the overlay agrees, confidence can increase.

If the technical setup is strong but the overlay is hostile, reduce size, wait for stronger confirmation, or avoid the trade.

If the overlay is bullish but the chart has no setup, there is no trade.

---

## ETF Flow Overlay

ETF flows help show whether institutional demand is supporting or weakening BTC.

### Bullish ETF Flow Signals
- Strong ETF net inflows
- Several consecutive days of ETF net inflows
- ETF inflows while BTC holds support
- ETF inflows while BTC reclaims a key level
- ETF inflows during acceptance above a range

### Bearish ETF Flow Signals
- Strong ETF net outflows
- Several consecutive days of ETF net outflows
- ETF outflows while BTC rejects resistance
- ETF outflows while BTC loses support
- ETF outflows during failed breakout attempts

### How to Use ETF Flows
ETF flows are best used for:
- Weekly bias
- Daily confidence
- Risk adjustment
- Confirming or questioning technical setups

ETF flows should not be used for:
- Exact entries
- Stop placement
- Ignoring support/resistance
- Chasing candles

---

## Funding Rate Overlay

Funding rates help show whether the perpetual futures market is balanced, crowded long, or crowded short.

### Bullish Funding Signals
- Neutral funding during bullish structure
- Negative funding at major support
- Negative funding after a liquidity sweep
- Funding reset after a flush
- Shorts crowded while price reclaims structure

### Bearish Funding Signals
- Extremely positive funding
- Longs crowded into resistance
- Positive funding while BTC fails to reclaim
- Positive funding during weakening structure
- Negative funding during clean breakdown continuation

### How to Use Funding Rates
Funding rates are best used for:
- Identifying overcrowded trades
- Spotting possible squeeze risk
- Filtering leveraged entries
- Deciding whether to reduce size

Funding is not automatically bullish or bearish by itself.

Context matters.

---

## Reserve Risk Overlay

Reserve Risk is a macro/cycle indicator.

It is used to judge long-term holder confidence relative to BTC price.

### Bullish Reserve Risk Signals
- Low Reserve Risk
- Reserve Risk rising from low levels
- Low Reserve Risk while weekly support holds
- Low Reserve Risk with improving market structure

### Bearish Reserve Risk Signals
- High Reserve Risk
- Reserve Risk reaching overheated areas
- High Reserve Risk while BTC rejects major resistance
- High Reserve Risk combined with weak market structure

### How to Use Reserve Risk
Reserve Risk is best used for:
- Monthly bias
- Weekly bias
- Macro cycle context
- Avoiding late-cycle overconfidence

Reserve Risk should not be used for:
- 1H entries
- 4H entries
- Exact exit timing
- Leveraged trade triggers

---

## Puell Multiple Overlay

The Puell Multiple helps judge miner revenue pressure and broader Bitcoin cycle conditions.

### Bullish Puell Multiple Signals
- Low Puell Multiple
- Puell Multiple rising from depressed levels
- Low Puell Multiple while BTC holds major support
- Miner stress reducing as price structure improves

### Bearish Puell Multiple Signals
- High Puell Multiple
- Very high Puell Multiple during euphoric price action
- High Puell Multiple while BTC rejects major resistance
- Elevated miner profitability combined with weakening structure

### How to Use Puell Multiple
The Puell Multiple is best used for:
- Macro context
- Cycle risk
- Weekly/monthly caution
- Spotting overheated market conditions

It should not be used as a standalone trading signal.

---

## Overlay Weighting

The overlay should have limited weight.

Suggested weighting:

| Component | Approximate Weight |
|---|---:|
| Price structure / support & resistance | 30% |
| Volume profile / POC / HVNs | 20% |
| Multi-timeframe confirmation | 20% |
| Acceptance / rejection / reclaim | 15% |
| Funding / ETF / on-chain overlay | 10-15% |
| Psychology / timing / weekend risk | Always active |

---

## Overlay Decision Rules

### Stronger Long Bias
A long setup improves when:
- BTC is holding technical support
- Higher timeframe structure is bullish
- ETF flows are positive
- Funding is neutral or not overheated
- On-chain cycle indicators are not flashing extreme caution

### Weaker Long Bias
A long setup weakens when:
- BTC is rejecting resistance
- ETF flows are negative
- Funding is extremely positive
- Longs appear overcrowded
- Macro/on-chain indicators look overheated

### Stronger Short Bias
A short setup improves when:
- BTC rejects resistance
- BTC fails to reclaim structure
- ETF flows are negative
- Funding is overheated to the long side
- On-chain cycle indicators show caution

### Weaker Short Bias
A short setup weakens when:
- BTC is holding major support
- ETF flows are positive
- Funding is negative and shorts are crowded
- Price reclaims structure after a liquidity sweep
- Macro/on-chain conditions are supportive

---

## Overlay Principle

The overlay is a bias tool, not a trade trigger.

No trade should be opened from ETF flows, funding rates, Reserve Risk, or Puell Multiple alone.

A trade still requires:
- A technical setup
- Confluence
- Confirmation
- Defined invalidation
- Controlled risk

# Support & Resistance

Support and resistance are the foundation of the framework.

These are the most important levels in the market.

## Horizontal Levels

Horizontal levels carry the highest importance because they represent:
- Historical reactions
- Market memory
- Liquidity concentration
- Institutional interest
- Repeated defended areas

These levels are considered stronger than diagonal trendlines.

---

# Volume Profile Analysis

Volume profile is one of the core components of the strategy.

The focus is heavily placed on:
- High Volume Nodes (HVNs)
- Point of Control (POC)
- Value Areas
- Acceptance and rejection zones

---

## High Volume Nodes (HVNs)

HVNs represent areas where:
- Large trading volume occurred
- The market spent significant time
- Buyers and sellers found agreement

These areas often become:
- Strong support
- Strong resistance
- Reaccumulation zones
- Distribution zones

---

## Point of Control (POC)

The Point of Control is one of the most important areas within the framework.

### Definition
The POC is:
- The price level with the highest traded volume within the selected range

### Why It Matters
The POC represents:
- Market acceptance
- Fair value
- Institutional participation
- High liquidity

Price often reacts strongly around the POC.

# Trendlines

The framework uses both:
- Horizontal trendlines
- Diagonal trendlines

However, they do not carry equal weight.

---

## Horizontal Trendlines
Highest importance.

Reason:
- More objective
- Based on historical price agreement
- Stronger market memory
- More reliable support/resistance

---

## Diagonal Trendlines
Secondary importance.

Used for:
- Trend direction
- Channels
- Momentum analysis
- Compression patterns
- Breakout structures

Diagonal trendlines are considered weaker than horizontal levels because:
- They are more subjective
- Easier to break
- Depend on interpretation and angle

A diagonal trendline alone is not enough for a trade.

# Range Trading Structure

The framework heavily relies on range analysis.

Markets often move:
- From one range into another
- From acceptance into expansion
- From support into resistance

---

## Example

### Range 1
102k - 106k

If BTC breaks above 106k and holds:
- Range 2 becomes active

### Range 2
106k - 112k

Key principle:
- Old resistance becomes support
- Acceptance above range = bullish
- Failure back into old range = weakness

---

# Acceptance vs Rejection

The strategy focuses heavily on:
- Market acceptance
- Market rejection

This is more important than individual candles.

---

## Bullish Acceptance
Signs:
- Holding support
- Strong reclaim
- Volume building above range
- Acceptance above key levels

Result:
Potential continuation higher

---

## Bearish Rejection
Signs:
- Rejection at resistance
- Failure reclaim
- Breakdown below support
- Weak momentum

Result:
Potential continuation lower

# Long Setup Framework

A long setup typically requires:

- Support holding
- Range support
- HVN or POC support
- Trendline support
- Higher timeframe bullish bias
- Structure reclaim
- Momentum confirmation
- ETF/funding/on-chain overlay supportive or neutral

---

# Short Setup Framework

A short setup typically requires:

- Resistance rejection
- Breakdown below support
- Failure reclaim
- Weak momentum
- Bearish higher timeframe structure
- Rejection from HVN or POC
- Trendline rejection
- ETF/funding/on-chain overlay hostile or cautionary

# Risk Management

Risk management is critical.

No level is guaranteed.

All levels are probability zones, not certainty zones.

---

# Core Risk Principles

- Capital preservation first
- Avoid emotional trading
- Avoid revenge trading
- Avoid overtrading
- Never force setups
- Wait for confirmation
- Use leverage carefully
- Do not trade from fundamental/on-chain signals alone
- Reduce size when the overlay conflicts with the technical setup

---

- Wins

- During high-conviction setups
- With strong confluence
- With defined invalidation
- With controlled risk
